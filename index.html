Finding the right hearing aid support shouldn't be complicated.

When a hearing aid stops working, most people start by asking around and hoping for the best. HearFix is being built to replace that guesswork with something clearer.

Finding the right specialist

Someone who is brilliant with one brand may not have the tools or training for another, and you often only find out after you've made the trip.

Knowing who to trust

Is this person a qualified professional? Can they service your model? Are they approved by the manufacturer? Those are three different questions, and you deserve a straight answer to each.

Coordinating support from abroad

If your mum or dad is in Nigeria and you're overseas, arranging a repair means a lot of phone calls and guesswork. You shouldn't have to chase it across time zones.

OUR STORY

Built by students, for everyone who depends on a hearing aid.

HearFix began with a question from a team of audiology students at the Federal University of Health Sciences, Ila-Orangun: when a hearing aid breaks, who can actually fix it?

Meet the team
Chapter 1

It started with a question.

We study audiology at the Federal University of Health Sciences, Ila-Orangun. In class we learn what a hearing aid gives a person: conversation, safety and independence, one small device at a time.

Then a practical question kept coming back. When that device stops working, who can actually fix it?

Chapter 2

The more we asked, the clearer the gap became.

A repairer who is excellent with one brand may not have the tools for another. Being qualified, being able to service a particular model and being approved by the manufacturer are three different things, yet people are rarely shown them separately.

And for the many Nigerians abroad who look after a parent from far away, arranging a repair means phone calls, guesswork and hope.

Chapter 3

So we started building HearFix.

HearFix helps people find suitable providers by brand, model and service, see exactly what each provider has been verified for, and follow a repair from first enquiry to collection. Families abroad can help, with the hearing aid user's consent.

It is an early prototype today: this website, dashboards for users, families and professionals, and a review workspace for approving providers.

What we promise

How we will behave as we grow.

Honest by design

We never invent providers, ratings or numbers. A status is shown only when someone has actually confirmed it.

Consent comes first

A family member acts only with the hearing aid user's permission, and they can withdraw it.

Nigeria first

Built around how hearing care is actually reached here, and for the families who support it from abroad.

Independent providers

HearFix connects people with professionals. We do not repair devices, and we do not replace clinical care.

What happens next

Where we are today.

HearFix is an early prototype. This is what is done, and what comes next.

  1. Done

    The idea

    Understanding the gap between hearing aid users and the people who can service their devices.

  2. Done

    The prototype

    This website, dashboards for users, families and professionals, and a review workspace, built to show how HearFix will work.

  3. Up next

    Listening to professionals

    Learning from audiologists, repair specialists and service centres about what they need from a platform like this.

  4. Planned

    A first pilot

    Testing HearFix with a small group of providers and families before we grow.

  5. Planned

    Growing the network

    Verifying more providers and reaching more families, step by step.
